Classify suspicious activity without confirmed access as P3 while investigating it.
Confirmed access to customer or personal data maps to P1 in the triage guide.
P2 is high priority for confirmed compromise needing containment, lateral movement, or privileged credential abuse.
Incident handling proceeds through preparation, detection and analysis, containment, eradication and recovery, then post-incident work.
