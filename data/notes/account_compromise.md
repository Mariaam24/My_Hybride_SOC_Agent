AD FS Event IDs 411 and 342 can mark incorrect credentials, while 516 marks an extranet lockout.
The investigation guide recommends verbose AD FS auditing and both success and failure audits.
Technique T1110 describes repetitive password guessing against accounts or acquired hashes.
